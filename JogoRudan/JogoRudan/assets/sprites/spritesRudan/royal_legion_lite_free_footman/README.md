# ROYAL LEGION LITE - Free 4-Direction Cartoon Footman + 3 Battle Effects

```text
ROYAL LEGION LITE - Free 4-Direction Cartoon Footman + 3 Battle Effects
by Tiny Tileset  |  https://tinytileset.itch.io

WHAT IS INSIDE
1 soldier (Footman with sword and round shield) with a WALK animation (8 frames) and an IDLE animation (8 frames) in 4 directions (down, left, right, up).
3 free effects (8 frames each, transparent PNG frames + a GIF each): sword slash, shield block and metal hit spark.
/animations        every frame as a PNG (plus sprite sheets) + animations.json (fps, frame size and ground point)
/rpg_maker_sheets  a 3x4 walk sheet (columns: step, stand, step; rows: down, left, right, up) - the classic RPG Maker layout
/bonus_combat_effects  the 3 effect animations (the slash faces RIGHT: flip or rotate it for other directions)

walk = 10 fps loop, idle = 6 fps loop. "ground_point_in_frame" in animations.json is the spot under the feet.

WANT MORE?
The full pack, ROYAL LEGION (6 soldiers: Footman, Spearman, Archer, Knight, Army Commander and Kingdom Hero with walk, idle and attack
in every direction, 20 battle effects, 60 icons and rigged parts), is at
https://tinytileset.itch.io
```
